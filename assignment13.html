Welcome to Computer Crisis! You are a computer technician responsible for diagnosing and repairing computers that have different technical problems.

How to Play

  • You will receive five different computer repair challenges.
  • Read the problem description and examine the symptoms.
  • Select the best solution from the available choices.
  • Correct answers earn 100 points.
  • Incorrect answers lose 25 points.
  • Complete all five challenges to see your final results.
