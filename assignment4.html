Assignment 4 - Gravitational Force Calculator

This calculator uses Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation to calculate the gravitational force between two objects. The results are also displayed on an interactive graph.

Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

F = G × (m₁ × m₂) / r²

F = gravitational force in Newtons
G = gravitational constant
m₁ = mass of object 1 in kilograms
m₂ = mass of object 2 in kilograms
r = distance between the objects in meters

The gravitational constant is G = 6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹.
